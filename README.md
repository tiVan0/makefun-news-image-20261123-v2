A picture-led magazine/atlas of the world's news of **Monday 23 November 2026** — one image, 1024×1024.

## The day

- **Wall Street roars back**: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; Nvidia +31.48% in a day, Nasdaq +6.51%, S&P 500 +4.73%, Dow +3.01%.
- **COP31 collapses** in Antalya — the longest climate summit ever ends on Sunday 22 November with no deal, the first collapse since COP15 in 2009.
- **Kyiv claims a Russian warship sunk off Libya**: seven Ukrainian sea drones hit the corvette Merkury and the cargo ship Angara at Benghazi; satellite imagery shows most of the Merkury capsized and sunk.
- **153 drones over Ukraine overnight**; 134 downed, 11 locations hit; 11 killed in Dymer, where a boy was pulled alive from the rubble after about 14 hours.

30 stories across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia and the wider world — every one drawn as an original illustration, with a labelled world map, a labelled BY THE NUMBERS strip, a three-line DATE CHECK strip, and a primary-source footer.

## How it is built

`render.py` (Pillow) draws `entry.png` at build time — every illustration is an original drawing, no photographs. `main.py` (standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
